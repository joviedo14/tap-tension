How to get clean readings
  1. Quiet room. Hold the racquet by the handle so the head hangs free, strings facing you.
  2. Hold the phone 5 to 15 cm from the strings, mic end toward them.
  3. Tap the center of the string bed sharply with something hard and round, like a pen or screwdriver handle. Not your finger, not a ball.
  4. Tap 5 or 6 times, a second apart. A spread under 3% means the taps agree.
  5. Measure right after stringing for your baseline, then every few sessions.

The reading is the string bed's average tension. It will usually sit 10 to 20% below what the machine was set to; that is normal. Track the drop from your first reading. Around 10 to 20% below that baseline is the restring zone.
